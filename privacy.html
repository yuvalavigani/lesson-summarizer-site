Privacy Policy – Lesson Summarizer

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who uses this app

Lesson Summarizer is a personal tool used only by its developer. It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Drive: the app reads lecture recordings from a single folder owned by the developer, and may upload the generated summary documents to that same Drive account.
  • Gmail: the app may send the generated summary to the developer's own email address.

How the data is used

Recordings are processed on the developer's own computer and, for transcription and summarization, may be sent to third-party AI services chosen by the developer. The data is used only to produce summaries for the developer.

Sharing and storage

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than the processing services above. Files are stored on the developer's computer and in the developer's own Google account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub page.
